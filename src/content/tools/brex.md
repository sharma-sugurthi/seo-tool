## What it does

Brex combines cards, reimbursements, travel booking and bill pay. Its AI assistant auto reviews expenses against policy, drafts memos and answers employee questions, so managers only see the exceptions.

## Where it shines

- Cards and local currency spend in many countries.
- Integrated travel booking so trips, receipts and policy live together.
- Good ERP integrations for NetSuite, QuickBooks and Sage.

## Where it falls short

- Startups with very little cash may not qualify for the card programme.
- Feature depth means a longer setup than a simple expense app.

## Verdict

The pick for global teams. If you are US only and card led, compare it head to head with Ramp.
