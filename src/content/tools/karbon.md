## What it does

Karbon combines shared email, tasks, client records and recurring work templates so a firm can see every client engagement in one place. Karbon AI drafts replies, summarises long email threads and suggests next steps.

## Where it shines

- Built for the way firms actually work: recurring monthly and annual jobs with many small tasks.
- Email triage inside the tool removes a lot of inbox switching.
- Client portal for document requests.

## Where it falls short

- Solo practitioners may find it more structure than they need.
- Migrating from spreadsheets and a shared inbox takes real setup time.

## Verdict

The leading choice for firms that want to run on process rather than memory.
