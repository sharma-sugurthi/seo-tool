## What it does

Tipalti handles the whole payables cycle for companies with global supplier bases: self service supplier onboarding, tax form collection, invoice capture and coding, approvals, and payments in local currencies with reconciliation back to the ERP.

## Where it shines

- Built for scale and cross border complexity that small business tools cannot handle.
- Tax compliance (W-9, W-8 and equivalents) collected automatically from suppliers.
- Strong controls and audit trail for finance teams preparing for audits.

## Where it falls short

- Implementation is a project, not an afternoon.
- Overkill for a company paying a few dozen domestic vendors.

## Verdict

A serious platform for marketplaces, ad networks and mid market companies with international supplier payments.
