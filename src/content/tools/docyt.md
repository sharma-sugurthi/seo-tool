## What it does

Docyt captures receipts and invoices, codes them, reconciles accounts and produces consolidated reports across locations, syncing to QuickBooks. It targets businesses with several entities and a lot of paper.

## Where it shines

- Multi entity roll ups that QuickBooks alone struggles with.
- Revenue reconciliation for hospitality and retail point of sale systems.
- Accounting firm partner programme.

## Where it falls short

- Onboarding is heavier than single entity tools.
- Interface feels dated next to newer entrants.

## Verdict

Useful for exactly the businesses it targets. Single location companies should look elsewhere.
