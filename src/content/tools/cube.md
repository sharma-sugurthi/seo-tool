## What it does

Cube connects accounting, HR and sales systems to a central model, then pushes and pulls that data from Excel and Google Sheets. Budgets, forecasts and board reports refresh without copy and paste, and AI helps summarise variances.

## Where it shines

- Works with Google Sheets as well as Excel, which many newer companies use.
- Faster to implement than traditional planning suites.
- Good fit for companies between 50 and 500 people.

## Where it falls short

- Very complex multi entity consolidation may need a heavier platform.
- Pricing is per company rather than a low self serve tier.

## Verdict

A practical step up for teams whose spreadsheets have become the system of record for planning.
