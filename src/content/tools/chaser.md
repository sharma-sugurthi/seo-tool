## What it does

Chaser connects to your accounting system, sends reminder emails and SMS on a schedule you control in your own tone, records every interaction, and uses payment history to predict late payers so you can act early.

## Where it shines

- Reminders read like they came from a person, which is why they work.
- Integrates with QuickBooks, Xero, Sage and others.
- Reporting on days sales outstanding you can show the board.

## Where it falls short

- It chases, it does not collect. Debt collection is a separate service.
- Small businesses with a handful of customers can do this by hand.

## Verdict

Pays for itself quickly if late payments are hurting cash flow.
