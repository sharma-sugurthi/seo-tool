## What it does

Intuit Assist sits inside QuickBooks Online and drafts transaction categories, reminds customers about overdue invoices, and answers plain English questions about the business ("how much did we spend on software last quarter?").

## Where it shines

- Nothing to integrate. If the books are already in QuickBooks, the AI is simply there.
- Bank feed categorisation suggestions improve quickly once you correct them a few times.
- Invoice reminders written by the assistant are noticeably better than the stock templates.

## Where it falls short

- Answers are only as good as the categorisation underneath. Messy books get confident wrong answers.
- Multi entity and consolidation are outside its comfort zone.
- Pricing tiers change often and the AI features are not on every plan.

## Verdict

If you run on QuickBooks Online, turn it on and use it. If you are choosing a ledger for a growing company, compare it against tools built for multi entity work first.
