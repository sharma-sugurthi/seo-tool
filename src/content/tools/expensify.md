## What it does

Employees photograph receipts, Expensify reads them, builds the report and routes it for approval. Reimbursement, corporate cards and invoicing are available in the same product.

## Where it shines

- Familiar. Many employees have used it at a previous job.
- Receipt scanning is quick and accurate on standard receipts.
- Free tier for very small teams.

## Where it falls short

- Card issuing and spend controls are less developed than card first platforms.
- Pricing and plan changes over the years have confused long time customers.

## Verdict

Still a solid choice for straightforward expense reporting. Compare it with card first tools if you want to remove expense reports entirely.
