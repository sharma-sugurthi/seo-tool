## What it does

Datarails connects to your ERP, CRM and spreadsheets, consolidates the data into a governed model and lets you keep working in Excel on top of it. Dashboards, variance analysis and a conversational AI layer sit over the consolidated data.

## Where it shines

- No retraining. The team keeps its Excel models and templates.
- Version control and an audit trail for spreadsheets that previously had none.
- Ask questions of the data in plain language.

## Where it falls short

- Quote based pricing aimed at mid market budgets.
- If your team wants to leave Excel behind entirely, other tools fit better.

## Verdict

Excellent for finance teams who love Excel and hate the manual consolidation that comes with it.
