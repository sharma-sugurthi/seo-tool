## What it does

JAX (Just Ask Xero) lets you create invoices, chase payments and ask about balances from chat, email or inside Xero itself. Bank reconciliation suggestions already use machine learning and get sharper as you accept them.

## Where it shines

- Clean interface and a large app marketplace, so the AI has good data to work with.
- Strong in the UK, Australia and New Zealand where Xero dominates small business accounting.
- Advisors can manage many clients from one practice view.

## Where it falls short

- US payroll and sales tax handling still rely on partners.
- JAX availability has rolled out region by region, so check it is live for you.

## Verdict

The best small business ledger for firms with international clients. The AI layer is useful, not magical, and improving each quarter.
