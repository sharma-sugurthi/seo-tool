## What it does

Anrok monitors where your sales create tax obligations, calculates the right tax at checkout through billing system integrations, registers you where needed, and files and remits returns. It is built specifically for software and digital goods.

## Where it shines

- Understands software specific rules that general tax engines often get wrong.
- Integrates with Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, QuickBooks and NetSuite among others.
- Nexus alerts before you cross a threshold, not after.

## Where it falls short

- Focused on software and digital sellers. Physical goods companies should look at general engines.
- A cost line that early stage companies sometimes defer for too long.

## Verdict

If you sell software into many jurisdictions, this is the category to budget for before the first audit letter arrives.
