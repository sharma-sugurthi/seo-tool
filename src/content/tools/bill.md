## What it does

BILL captures incoming invoices by email or upload, extracts the data, routes approvals and pays vendors by ACH, card or check. It syncs to QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite and Sage Intacct and has a large accountant partner network.

## Where it shines

- The default AP tool for thousands of accounting firms, so your bookkeeper probably already knows it.
- Approval workflows that work on a phone.
- Receivables side included, so invoicing and collections live in the same place.

## Where it falls short

- Per user pricing adds up for larger teams.
- Data extraction still needs a human check on unusual invoice layouts.

## Verdict

The safe choice for small business AP. If you process thousands of invoices a month, look at enterprise tools instead.
