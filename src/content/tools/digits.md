## What it does

Digits rebuilt the ledger with AI at the centre. Transactions are categorised and reconciled continuously, reports update as data lands, and the assistant explains variances in plain language.

## Where it shines

- Very fast close. Founders see current numbers instead of last month's.
- Shareable, interactive reports that investors and boards actually read.
- Built with accounting firms as partners, not adversaries.

## Where it falls short

- Younger ecosystem than QuickBooks or Xero, so fewer third party apps.
- Switching ledgers is never trivial. Plan the migration.

## Verdict

The most credible attempt at an AI first ledger today. Worth a look if you are choosing fresh or frustrated with a slow close.
