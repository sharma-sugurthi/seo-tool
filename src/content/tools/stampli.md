## What it does

Stampli centres every invoice in a shared conversation between AP, approvers and vendors. Its AI assistant suggests GL codes and approvers, flags duplicates and learns from corrections. It connects to a long list of ERPs without requiring changes to them.

## Where it shines

- Fast deployment relative to other mid market AP tools.
- The communication layer on each invoice cuts email back and forth.
- Broad ERP coverage including older systems.

## Where it falls short

- Payments are an add on rather than the core.
- Pricing is quote based, so budgeting requires a sales conversation.

## Verdict

A good fit when the ERP is staying put and the pain is approvals and questions, not payments.
