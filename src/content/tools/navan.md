## What it does

Navan combines a travel booking tool with expense management and corporate cards. Because it knows the trip, it can match charges, receipts and policy without asking the traveller, and it rebooks or refunds when plans change.

## Where it shines

- Travel and expenses genuinely integrated rather than bolted together.
- Policy enforced at booking time, which prevents out of policy spend instead of catching it later.
- Free tier for small companies.

## Where it falls short

- If your team rarely travels, much of the product is unused.
- Some users prefer booking directly with airlines and hotels for loyalty reasons.

## Verdict

The tool to evaluate first if flights and hotels dominate your expense reports.
