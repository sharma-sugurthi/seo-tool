## What it does

Botkeeper sits on top of QuickBooks and Xero client files, categorises transactions, reconciles accounts and flags exceptions for the firm's staff to resolve. It is sold to firms, not directly to small businesses.

## Where it shines

- Designed around the firm's workflow across many client files at once.
- Reduces the junior staff hours per client.
- Firm branded client experience.

## Where it falls short

- Not available to a business that wants to buy it directly.
- Quality depends on how well the client's data feeds are set up.

## Verdict

Worth evaluating for firms with 30 or more bookkeeping clients and a hiring bottleneck.
