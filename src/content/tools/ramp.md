## What it does

Ramp issues cards and then automates everything that happens after the swipe: receipt matching, memo drafting, policy checks, GL coding and sync to your ledger. Ramp Intelligence flags price increases in vendor contracts and duplicate subscriptions.

## Where it shines

- Free core product funded by interchange, with paid tiers for larger teams.
- Closes the books faster because expenses arrive coded and approved.
- Vendor and price benchmarking data nobody else offers at this scale.

## Where it falls short

- US only for cards.
- Works best when everyone in the company is on Ramp cards. Mixed environments lose much of the automation.

## Verdict

If you are a US company eligible for Ramp, it is the fastest way to remove expense work from the finance team.
