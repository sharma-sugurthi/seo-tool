## What it does

Puzzle connects bank, card, payroll and billing systems and produces accrual financials continuously, with AI categorisation and a metrics layer for burn, runway and revenue.

## Where it shines

- Generous free tier for very early companies.
- Accrual accounting done properly, which most startup tools skip.
- Startup metrics next to the ledger, so no separate dashboard tool.

## Where it falls short

- Designed for startups. Traditional small businesses may find the metrics irrelevant.
- Complex inventory or multi entity is not the focus.

## Verdict

A strong free to cheap starting point for a funded startup that wants clean books from day one.
