## What it does

Vic.ai reads incoming invoices, codes them to the right GL account and cost centre, routes approvals and pushes to your ERP with high straight through processing rates. Trained on hundreds of millions of invoices rather than templates.

## Where it shines

- High autonomy rate once tuned, which means AP clerks review exceptions instead of every invoice.
- Native integrations with NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Dynamics and other mid market ERPs.
- Strong audit trail for SOX and external audit.

## Where it falls short

- Not for small businesses. Implementation takes weeks and pricing reflects that.
- Value depends on invoice volume. Below a few hundred a month the payback stretches out.

## Verdict

A serious AP automation platform for finance teams that have outgrown inbox and spreadsheet approvals.
