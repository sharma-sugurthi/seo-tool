A new finance tool has no reviews, no backlinks and no mentions anywhere ChatGPT or Google might look. The fastest way to fix all three at once is to get listed where buyers and search engines already go. Here is the list we work from, grouped by what each place is actually good for. All of these are free at the basic level.

## Tier 1: the ones that get cited by AI answers

These show up repeatedly as sources in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for "best tools" questions. List here first.

| Site | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| G2 | Reviews and comparison pages are quoted constantly. Claim the profile and get five honest reviews. |
| Capterra and GetApp | Same owner, same effect. One submission covers both. |
| Product Hunt | A launch gives you a permanent page with a strong link and a burst of attention. |
| AlternativeTo | Appears for every "alternatives to X" query. List yourself as an alternative to the incumbents. |
| SaaSHub | Comparison pages for almost every software category. |

## Tier 2: AI tool directories

High volume of listings, modest individual value, but together they build the citation footprint that AI models learn from.

- There's An AI For That
- Futurepedia
- TopAI.tools
- Toolify
- AI Tools Directory
- Insidr.ai
- Foundr AI
- [Our own directory](/submit-tool/), which is finance specific

## Tier 3: startup and founder communities

Good for early users and a few strong links.

- BetaList for pre launch and early stage products
- Indie Hackers product pages
- Uneed and Launching Next for launch day traffic
- StartupStash
- Crunchbase, for the profile that journalists and investors check

## Tier 4: where finance buyers actually talk

Not directories, but where the honest mentions that AI answers love come from. Show up as a person, answer questions, and mention the product only when it is the real answer.

- r/Accounting, r/Bookkeeping, r/smallbusiness, r/startups
- QuickBooks Community and Xero Central, if you integrate with either
- Accounting focused Slack groups and the AICPA forums
- LinkedIn, where accountants and finance leaders decide who to trust

## Tier 5: review the agency side too

If you sell services rather than software, Clutch, GoodFirms and DesignRush are the equivalents of G2. They are slower to earn reviews on, but they rank well for "best X agency" searches.

## How to do this in a day

1. Prepare one folder: logo in three sizes, a 140 character tagline, a 300 word description, five screenshots and a demo video link.
2. Create a spreadsheet with a row per site: date submitted, status, live URL.
3. Submit Tier 1 in the morning. Each takes 15 to 30 minutes done properly.
4. Submit Tiers 2 and 3 in the afternoon. Most take five minutes.
5. Spend the following weeks on Tier 4. That is the work that compounds.

We do this as a [service](/services/) for companies that would rather not spend the day. Either way, the spreadsheet is the asset: it is how you prove to yourself, and later to a buyer of the company, where every mention came from.
