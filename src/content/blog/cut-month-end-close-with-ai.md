Most finance teams do not need a new ledger to close faster. They need the work that happens before the ledger to arrive clean. Here is the order we recommend, with the AI tools that fit each step. Each step stands alone, so stop whenever the close is fast enough.

## Day 1: stop chasing receipts

The slowest part of any close is waiting on people. Move card spend to a platform that captures receipts and memos at the point of purchase. [Ramp](/tools/ramp/) and [Brex](/tools/brex/) both text the cardholder the moment a charge lands and match the receipt automatically. Expected saving: two to three days of chasing.

## Day 2 to 3: let categorisation happen continuously

Turn on the AI categorisation in your ledger and correct it daily for the first month. [Intuit Assist](/tools/quickbooks-intuit-assist/), [Xero](/tools/xero-jax/) and [Digits](/tools/digits/) all learn from corrections. The goal is that by month end there is nothing left to categorise, only exceptions to review.

## Day 3 to 4: reconcile as you go

Bank reconciliation should be a five minute daily habit, not a month end event. AI matching gets most of it. Anything unmatched after 48 hours becomes a task for a named person.

## Day 5: automate the boring accruals

Recurring accruals, prepaid amortisation and depreciation should be templates that post themselves. Most ledgers support this natively. The AI part is the variance check: ask the assistant "what changed more than 15 percent versus last month" and review those lines first.

## Day 6: build the review pack once

Create a report set (P&L versus budget, balance sheet, cash, AR ageing, AP ageing) and share it as a live link rather than a PDF. [Digits](/tools/digits/) and [Puzzle](/tools/puzzle/) do this well. Reviewers comment in the report and you fix at the source.

## What to measure

| Metric | Before | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Business days to close | 10 | 3 |
| Transactions coded automatically | 40 percent | 90 percent |
| Receipts missing at close | 60 | under 5 |
| Reviewer questions per close | 25 | under 8 |

## What not to automate

Revenue recognition judgement, related party transactions and anything the auditor has flagged before. Automate the volume, keep the judgement.
