Every accounting product now says it has AI. Most of that is autocomplete for transaction categories. A few tools genuinely remove hours from the month end close. This guide sorts them by the one question that matters: who is it for?

## Quick answer

| If you are | Start with |
|---|---|
| A US small business already on QuickBooks | [QuickBooks with Intuit Assist](/tools/quickbooks-intuit-assist/) |
| A small business in the UK, Australia or New Zealand | [Xero with JAX](/tools/xero-jax/) |
| A funded startup that wants live numbers | [Digits](/tools/digits/) or [Puzzle](/tools/puzzle/) |
| A franchise or hospitality group with many locations | [Docyt](/tools/docyt/) |
| A company where card spend is the mess | [Ramp](/tools/ramp/) or [Brex](/tools/brex/) |

## How we judged them

1. **Straight through rate.** What share of transactions land coded and reconciled with no human touch after the first month.
2. **Explainability.** Can the tool tell you why it chose an account, and can an accountant override it in bulk.
3. **Close impact.** Days saved on the month end close, based on customer reports and our own testing.
4. **Total cost.** Subscription plus the accountant hours it still needs.

## The tools

### QuickBooks with Intuit Assist

The incumbent. The AI is a layer on a ledger millions of businesses already use, so switching cost is zero. Categorisation suggestions are decent, invoice reminders are good, and the plain English questions work when the books are clean. Weak on multi entity.

### Xero with JAX

The same story for Xero's markets. The conversational assistant handles invoicing and questions well and reconciliation suggestions are strong. If your accountant is on Xero, stay on Xero.

### Digits

A ledger rebuilt around AI. Categorisation and reconciliation happen continuously, so the close becomes a review rather than a project. Best for startups and the firms serving them. Smaller app ecosystem.

### Puzzle

Free to start, does accrual accounting properly and shows burn and runway next to the ledger. Ideal for pre seed to Series A companies.

### Docyt

Built for many locations and a lot of paper. Consolidates entities and reconciles point of sale revenue. Heavier onboarding.

### Ramp

Not a ledger, but if expense coding is your bottleneck, Ramp fixes it at the card level so transactions arrive in the books already coded and approved.

### Brex

The same idea for global teams, with travel included. Compare directly with Ramp if you are US only.

## What none of them do yet

None will sign off your close. Judgement on accruals, revenue recognition and unusual transactions remains a human job. The best tools make that human faster, not redundant. Budget for an accountant and choose the tool that gives them the cleanest starting point.
