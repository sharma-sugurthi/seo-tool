# Launch plan: one action a day

Do the day's item, tick it, stop. Days are working days. If an item takes ten minutes, spend the rest of the time writing an article. Two articles a week for the first two months matters more than anything else on this list.

## Days 1 to 10: ship

- [ ] Day 1. Buy the domain (lantle.ai or a .com fallback). Point it at Cloudflare.
- [ ] Day 2. Update `src/site.config.ts`: URL, email, LinkedIn link. Update `public/robots.txt`. Push.
- [ ] Day 3. Create a free Formspree form, paste the endpoint into `formEndpoint`, send yourself a test from every form on the site.
- [ ] Day 4. Deploy to Cloudflare Pages (build `npm run build`, output `dist`). Add the domain, turn on HTTPS.
- [ ] Day 5. Google Search Console: verify the domain, submit `sitemap-index.xml`. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console.
- [ ] Day 6. Fill in the "Who is behind it" paragraph on the About page with your own details. No photo needed, but a real name and a LinkedIn link.
- [ ] Day 7. Set up a PayPal Business account so you can send invoices. Bank transfer details in a note for larger clients.
- [ ] Day 8. Create the Lantle LinkedIn page. Post the "Where to list an AI finance tool for free" article as the first post.
- [ ] Day 9. Create a Google Business Profile and a Clutch profile for the services side.
- [ ] Day 10. Write and publish article number 6. From here on, two a week.

## Days 11 to 30: build authority (the same play aibusinesstool.com ran, done properly)

Keep a spreadsheet: site, date submitted, status, live URL. This spreadsheet is also the deliverable for your directory placement service, so build it carefully.

- [ ] Day 11. Product Hunt: create the maker profile and the product page for Lantle. Do not launch yet.
- [ ] Day 12. G2 and Capterra: check whether a publication qualifies. If not, skip and note it.
- [ ] Day 13. SaaSHub and AlternativeTo listings.
- [ ] Day 14. There's An AI For That, Futurepedia and TopAI.tools.
- [ ] Day 15. Toolify, AI Tools Directory, Insidr.ai, Foundr AI.
- [ ] Day 16. BetaList, Uneed, Launching Next, StartupStash.
- [ ] Day 17. Indie Hackers product page, Crunchbase profile, F6S.
- [ ] Day 18. Medium account. Republish one article with a canonical link back to Lantle.
- [ ] Day 19. LinkedIn article: republish a second post with a link back.
- [ ] Day 20. Sign up for Featured (formerly HARO) and Qwoted. Answer one finance or AI request.
- [ ] Day 21. Product Hunt launch day. Ask ten people to leave honest comments.
- [ ] Day 22. Join the QuickBooks Community and Xero Central. Answer two questions well, no links.
- [ ] Day 23. Join two accounting Slack groups. Introduce yourself, no pitch.
- [ ] Day 24. Answer three Featured or Qwoted requests.
- [ ] Day 25. Email five tools you have listed. Tell them they are listed, ask for a screenshot and a correction if needed. One in five links back.
- [ ] Day 26. GoodFirms and DesignRush profiles for the services side.
- [ ] Day 27. Check Search Console for the first indexed pages and impressions. Fix anything flagged.
- [ ] Day 28. Second batch of five "you are listed" emails.
- [ ] Day 29. Republish a third article on Medium with canonical link.
- [ ] Day 30. Check domain rating with the free Ahrefs checker. Write the number down. Target: 10 by day 60, 20 by day 90.

## Days 11 to 30, in parallel: first paying clients

Sell services now, placements later. The site is your portfolio, not yet your product.

- [ ] Fiverr and Upwork gigs for: directory submission (100 directories), guest post placement, LinkedIn ghostwriting, SEO article writing, AI visibility audit.
- [ ] Do the directory submission for Lantle first. Screenshot the spreadsheet as proof of work in the gig.
- [ ] Offer the AI visibility audit at the founding price to 10 fintech or accounting SaaS companies on LinkedIn. Two out of ten usually say yes. Upsell into content or placements.
- [ ] Join three Facebook groups and two Slack communities where SaaS marketers buy guest posts and links. Listen for a week before offering anything.

## Day 60 onward: sell placements on the site

- [ ] List the site on Adsy, Collaborator, Getalink and WhitePress once domain rating passes 20.
- [ ] Add domain rating, monthly visitors and audience description to the Advertise page. Real numbers only.
- [ ] Cap sponsored posts at one in four articles. Keep publishing your own.
- [ ] Reply to every enquiry within a day. Payment before publication, always.
- [ ] Track: enquiries, close rate, average order, repeat buyers. Raise prices when you are turning down work.

## Later, when there is traffic

- Newsletter. Not before there are readers to sign up.
- Stripe or another card processor. PayPal invoices are fine for the first twenty orders.
- Reddit presence as a service. Only once you have run your own account well for a few months.

## Rules that keep the site alive

- Label sponsored content and use rel="sponsored". Google's site reputation abuse policy targets unlabelled third party content.
- Decline off niche buyers (casino, crypto trading, adult, loans, essays). One of them can cost you the domain.
- No fake accounts or vote rings for clients, anywhere. Ban risk lands on the client and your reputation.
- Real author names on everything.
